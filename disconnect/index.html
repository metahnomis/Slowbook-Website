Disconnect Slowbook from QuickBooks Online

Follow these steps to disconnect Slowbook, also known as Importateur de devis QuickBooks.

Disconnection instructions

  1. Open the Slowbook desktop application.
  2. Click the settings gear.
  3. Click Disconnect QuickBooks.
  4. To revoke access completely, sign in to your Intuit account and remove Slowbook from the connected-app settings.
  5. If assistance is required, email info@metahnomis.ca.

Important: This public page provides instructions only. It does not disconnect an account or collect Intuit credentials.

You may use the connection instructions if you later need to reconnect Slowbook.
