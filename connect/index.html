Connect Slowbook to QuickBooks Online

Slowbook, also known as Importateur de devis QuickBooks, is a locally installed internal business application. Only employees authorized by their company should connect it to the company’s QuickBooks Online account.

Connection instructions

  1. Open the Slowbook desktop application.
  2. Select Production.
  3. Click Connect to QuickBooks.
  4. Sign in to Intuit using an account authorized to access the company’s QuickBooks Online data.
  5. Select the correct QuickBooks company.
  6. Review the authorization screen and approve access.
  7. After the confirmation page appears, return to Slowbook. The connected company name should be displayed.

Important: This public page provides instructions only. It is not the OAuth callback and does not collect Intuit credentials.
