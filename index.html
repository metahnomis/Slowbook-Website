Slowbook

Importateur de devis QuickBooks

Slowbook is an internal desktop utility that converts an Excel or CSV file into a QuickBooks Online estimate. It is distributed directly to authorized company employees and is not operated as a public web application.

How to obtain or open Slowbook

Ask your company administrator for the approved installer. If Slowbook is already installed on your company-managed computer, open the Slowbook desktop application from the location provided by your administrator.

Only employees who are authorized by their company may connect Slowbook to the company’s QuickBooks Online account.
